Organic chemistry studies molecules built around carbon — alkanes, alcohols, carbonyls, aromatics, heterocycles, polymers, natural products — and the reactions that turn one into another. Its methodological core is *synthesis*: planning and executing the sequence of bond-making and bond-breaking steps that converts cheap, available starting materials into a target structure, ideally with control over connectivity, stereochemistry, and yield. Modern frontiers focus on catalysts that enable bond constructions previously thought impossible — asymmetric transformations, photoredox catalysis, transition-metal-mediated cross-couplings, late-stage functionalisation — and on integrating computation and machine learning into reaction design.
