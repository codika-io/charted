**Molecular Mechanics and Force Fields** — Classical force fields — AMBER, CHARMM, OPLS, GAFF — and their parametrization.

The field organises around several methodological axes: how the underlying objects are modelled, how they are measured, how they are connected to the rest of chemistry, and which empirical phenomena drive open questions. ## Foundations and core methods

A primary reference for this area is *Molecular Modelling: Principles and Applications* (Leach, 2001), which lays out the core concepts that govern molecular mechanics and force fields. The treatment frames the subject within the broader context of theoretical and computational chemistry and motivates the conceptual vocabulary used throughout this page. ## Open questions

Open methodological questions in molecular mechanics and force fields include the transferability of the standard methods to harder regimes, the integration of newer measurement and modelling tools, and the connection to neighbouring subfields of theoretical and computational chemistry.
