## Foundations and core methods

A primary reference for this area is *Biochemistry* (Voet and Voet, 2016), which lays out the core concepts that govern dna and rna structure. The treatment frames the subject within the broader context of nucleic acid chemistry and motivates the conceptual vocabulary used throughout this page. The discussion here cites this work as a general anchor rather than for a specific claim, since the exact contribution claim is treated cautiously in line with the Charted sourcing policy.

A complementary perspective comes from *Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry* (Nelson and Cox, 2021), which provides further background on the methods and results most relevant to dna and rna structure. Together with the previous reference, it establishes the standard expectations for how practitioners approach the topic in current practice.

Open methodological questions in dna and rna structure include the transferability of the standard methods to harder regimes, the integration of newer measurement and modelling tools, and the connection to neighbouring subfields of nucleic acid chemistry.
