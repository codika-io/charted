## Foundations and core methods

A primary reference for this area is *Modern Quantum Chemistry: Introduction to Advanced Electronic Structure Theory* (Szabo and Ostlund, 1996), which lays out the core concepts that govern post-hartree–fock methods. The treatment frames the subject within the broader context of quantum chemistry and motivates the conceptual vocabulary used throughout this page. Open methodological questions in post-hartree–fock methods include the transferability of the standard methods to harder regimes, the integration of newer measurement and modelling tools, and the connection to neighbouring subfields of quantum chemistry.
