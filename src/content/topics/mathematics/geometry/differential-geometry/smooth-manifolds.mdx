**Smooth Manifolds and Tensor Fields.** Charts, atlases, tangent bundles, and differential forms.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of smooth manifolds and tensor fields approach the subject from complementary angles. Lee, *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds* (2013) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to. Spivak, *A Comprehensive Introduction to Differential Geometry* (1999) gives a parallel, more proof-oriented exposition of the same material and is widely used as a graduate text.

Open methodological questions for smooth manifolds and tensor fields include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
