**Topological Quantum Field Theory.** Atiyah's axioms, Chern–Simons theory, and quantum invariants.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of topological quantum field theory approach the subject from complementary angles. Deligne, *Quantum Fields and Strings: A Course for Mathematicians* (1999) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to.
