**Surrogate Modeling for UQ.** Gaussian processes, radial basis functions, and neural surrogates.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of surrogate modeling for uq approach the subject from complementary angles. Forrester, *Engineering Design via Surrogate Modelling* (2008) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to.

Open methodological questions for surrogate modeling for uq include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
