**Interest Rate Models.** Heath–Jarrow–Morton and LIBOR market models.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of interest rate models approach the subject from complementary angles. Brigo, *Interest Rate Models — Theory and Practice* (2007) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to.

Open methodological questions for interest rate models include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
