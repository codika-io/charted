**Convex Optimization.** Duality, KKT conditions, and interior-point methods.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of convex optimization approach the subject from complementary angles. Boyd, *Convex Optimization* (2004) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to. Rockafellar, *Convex Analysis* (1970) provides historical context and an early systematic exposition of the material.

Open methodological questions for convex optimization include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
