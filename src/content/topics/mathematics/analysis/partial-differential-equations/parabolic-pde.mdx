**Parabolic PDE.** Heat equation, semigroup theory, and reaction-diffusion systems.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of parabolic pde approach the subject from complementary angles. Ladyzhenskaya, *Linear and Quasilinear Equations of Parabolic Type* (1968) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to. Evans, *Partial Differential Equations* (2010) gives a parallel, more proof-oriented exposition of the same material and is widely used as a graduate text.

Open methodological questions for parabolic pde include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
