**Nonlinear Dispersive PDE.** Schrödinger, KdV, and wave maps; scattering and blowup.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of nonlinear dispersive pde approach the subject from complementary angles. Tao, *Nonlinear Dispersive Equations: Local and Global Analysis* (2006) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to.

Open methodological questions for nonlinear dispersive pde include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
