**Graph Limits and Graphons.** Lovász's theory of dense graph limits.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of graph limits and graphons approach the subject from complementary angles. Lovasz, *Large Networks and Graph Limits* (2012) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to.

Open methodological questions for graph limits and graphons include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
