**Graph Coloring.** Chromatic number, list coloring, and the four color theorem. This page collects canonical references that organise the subject and provide entry points to its main techniques.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of graph coloring approach the subject from complementary angles. Chartrand, *Chromatic Graph Theory* (2008) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to.

Open methodological questions for graph coloring include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
