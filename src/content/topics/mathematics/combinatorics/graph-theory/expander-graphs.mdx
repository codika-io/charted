**Expander Graphs.** Spectral, combinatorial, and algebraic expanders; Ramanujan graphs.

## Recent technical contributions

A handful of recent papers carry the methodological frontier of expander graphs forward. *Expander graphs and their applications* (Hoory et al., 2006) is a primary reference for this area and develops new techniques or results that downstream work builds on.

Open methodological questions for expander graphs include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
