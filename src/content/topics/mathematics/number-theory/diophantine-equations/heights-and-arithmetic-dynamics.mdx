**Heights and Arithmetic Dynamics.** Weil heights and dynamical analogs of Diophantine geometry.

## Foundations and canonical references

The standard treatments of heights and arithmetic dynamics approach the subject from complementary angles. Silverman, *The Arithmetic of Dynamical Systems* (2007) is the anchor reference for the subject and lays out the core definitions, theorems, and worked examples that practitioners return to.

Open methodological questions for heights and arithmetic dynamics include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
