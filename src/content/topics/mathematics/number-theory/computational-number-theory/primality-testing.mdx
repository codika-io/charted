**Primality Testing.** AKS, Miller–Rabin, and elliptic curve primality proving.

## Supporting and adjacent work

A number of supporting contributions sharpen specific aspects of primality testing or connect it to neighbouring problems. *PRIMES is in P* (Agrawal et al., 2004) contributes to this area as one of the supporting references that inform current practice.

Open methodological questions for primality testing include sharpening the bridges between foundational theory and computational practice, extending classical results to broader or more structured settings, and integrating the techniques surveyed above with adjacent mathematical disciplines. The references listed in this page are the entry points that current work builds on.
