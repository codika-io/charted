Genetics traces inheritance and variation; genomics scales the same questions to whole genomes and whole populations. The field's methodological frontier is statistical: how to associate millions of variants with thousands of phenotypes, how to assemble reference genomes that capture diversity beyond a single individual, and how to turn association signals into individual-level risk predictions. Frontiers cluster around GWAS, polygenic prediction, population genomics, and pangenome assembly.
