Cell biology asks how the molecular components characterised by biochemistry and molecular biology assemble into a working cell — its compartments, cytoskeleton, signalling cascades, and life cycle. Modern cell biology has been reshaped by single-cell measurement, live imaging at organelle resolution, and three-dimensional culture systems that recreate tissue context in vitro. Frontiers cluster around single-cell omics, organoid models, autophagy, and cell-cycle control.
