Molecular biology studies how genetic information is stored, copied, expressed, and regulated at the level of individual macromolecules. Its methodological core sits between biochemistry and genetics: dissect a pathway by perturbing one molecule at a time and reading the consequences with sequencing, mass spectrometry, or imaging. Frontiers cluster around programmable genome editing, RNA biology, transcriptional regulation, and the repair machinery that maintains genomic integrity.
