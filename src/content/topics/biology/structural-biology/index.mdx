Structural biology resolves how proteins, nucleic acids, and their assemblies fold and rearrange. The discipline's last decade has been dominated by cryo-electron microscopy, which made near-atomic structures of flexible complexes routine, and by deep-learning structure prediction, which closed a fifty-year gap between sequence and structure. Frontiers cluster around cryo-EM workflows, predicted-structure pipelines, integrative modelling that fuses heterogeneous data, and cryo-electron tomography of intact cellular contexts.
