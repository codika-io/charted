**Tumor Microenvironment** sits within cancer biology and addresses stromal, immune, and vascular components shaping tumor behavior. The page below sketches the conceptual scope of the area, the methodological tools it relies on, and the recent literature anchoring its current frontier.

*Weinberg, The Biology of Cancer* is a standard reference for the foundations covered here, used across the field to anchor terminology, canonical models, and the relationships between sub-areas of tumor microenvironment. Treat it as the entry point to which the more specialised work below adds frontier detail.

Open questions in tumor microenvironment cluster around scaling current methods to larger systems, integrating measurements across modalities, and producing predictive rather than descriptive models.
