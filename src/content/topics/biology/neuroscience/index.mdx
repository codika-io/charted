Neuroscience studies the cells, circuits, and computations of nervous systems. Its modern frontier is methodological: reconstructing complete wiring diagrams from volumetric electron microscopy, perturbing defined neuron populations with light-activated channels, and modelling neural computation with quantitative theories that connect single-cell recordings to behaviour. Frontiers cluster around connectomics, optogenetics, computational neuroscience, and neural decoding.
