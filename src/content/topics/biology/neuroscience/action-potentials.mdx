**Action Potentials and Excitability** sits within cellular neuroscience and addresses hodgkin–huxley framework, ion-channel biophysics, and spike generation. The page below sketches the conceptual scope of the area, the methodological tools it relies on, and the recent literature anchoring its current frontier.

The area organises around a small number of recurring axes: *scope* (what biological scales the work spans), *method* (the dominant experimental or computational tools), *data regime* (what kinds of measurements are now routine vs. still frontier), and *open questions* (what the field cannot yet do reliably). The sources below cover different combinations of these axes.

## Foundational references

*Hille, Ion Channels of Excitable Membranes* is a standard reference for the foundations covered here, used across the field to anchor terminology, canonical models, and the relationships between sub-areas of action potentials and excitability. Treat it as the entry point to which the more specialised work below adds frontier detail.

Open questions in action potentials and excitability cluster around scaling current methods to larger systems, integrating measurements across modalities, and producing predictive rather than descriptive models.
